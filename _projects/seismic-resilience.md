I investigate how structural systems, seismic protection, and reliable response information can improve earthquake performance. This work ranges from sacrificial-energy dissipation outriggers for tall buildings to intelligent base-isolation design and buckling-restrained brace retrofits for reinforced-concrete frames.

The studies connect physical mechanisms with learning-based methods: replaceable energy-dissipating components control structural damage, physics- and rule-guided generative models support isolation design, and generative search is coupled with retrofit optimization. A convolutional neural network identifies environmental vibrations to select optimized linear quadratic regulator parameters for adaptive vibration control. Research on time-frequency response identification and accelerometer uncertainty examines the quality of the measurements and assessments used to understand structural behavior.

## Research focus

- **Seismic protection:** sacrificial energy dissipation, isolation bearings, and brace-based retrofits.
- **Intelligent retrofit design:** generation of candidate layouts followed by structural optimization.
- **Adaptive vibration control:** convolutional neural network-based vibration identification and selection of optimized linear quadratic regulator parameters.
- **Response and uncertainty:** deep-transfer-learning identification and sensor effects on damage assessment.
